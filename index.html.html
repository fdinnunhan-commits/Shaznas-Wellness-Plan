Nutrition Framework

Daily Energy Target

1,450 - 1,500 kcal/day

A gentle deficit to promote fat loss without triggering hunger, lethargy, or acid build-up.

The 50/50 Rice Bowl

The ultimate strategy for volume eaters who love rice.

How to: Mix 1/2 cup cooked rice thoroughly with 1/2 cup steamed, shredded cabbage. Visually doubles the portion while slashing dense calories.

The 6 Hidden Calorie Traps

Sri Lankan cooking is healthy, but these specific elements must be strictly controlled to stay within your deficit.

1. Thel Dala (Tempering)

Never pour from the bottle. Measure exactly max 1 tsp oil per curry pot. Use water splashes if sticking.

2. Grated Coconut (Pol)

Ban this from daily mallum. Replace entirely with fresh lime juice, tomatoes, and onions for a massive calorie save.

3. Thick Coconut Milk (Miti Kiri)

Use only the second/third squeeze (thin milk/vathura) for gravies. Avoid the thick, fatty cream.

4. Pre-Frying Ingredients

No deep-fried eggplants or fish before currying. Bake, air-fry, or cook directly in the broth instead.

5. Liquid Sugar

Skip the sugar or jaggery in your daily tea. Spiking insulin will stop fat burning and crash your energy.

6. Deep-Fried Papadams

Replace the crunch of fried papadam with fresh, water-rich cucumber slices or lime-soaked onions.

Menu & Preparation

Sunday Weekly Meal Prep (1.5 Hours)

1

Carb Base

Cook rice. Finely shred and lightly steam cabbage. Store separately to create the 50/50 bowl daily.

2

Volume Proteins

Cook lean chicken, beef, or Soya meat. Add equal parts chopped Pathola or Watakolu into the curry to absorb gravy and double the size.

3

Dinner Base

Boil a large batch of soupy Parippu (dhal) with turmeric and garlic. Store plain; you will add fresh spinach right before eating.

4

Snacks & Extras

Boil Mun Ata (green gram) until soft. Dice fresh Papaya. Dice a batch of onions and tomatoes for morning eggs.

The Daily Menu Pattern

  • Wake Up
    Warm water, followed by plain tea or soothing herbal Kenda (prevents early acid build-up).
  • Breakfast
    2 whole eggs + 2 egg whites (soft scrambled with tomatoes). Served with 2 slices bread or 5 string hoppers.
  • Lunch
    The 50/50 Bowl: Rice & Cabbage mix. Massive portion of Soya/Chicken + Pathola curry. Side of Gotukola mallum (lime dressing, NO coconut).
  • Snack
    Bowl of diced Papaya (aids digestion) or soft-boiled Mun Ata.
  • Dinner
    Warm soupy dhal. Bring to simmer and toss in fresh spinach (Nivithi). Serve with a small roti. (Light and warm for perfect digestion before bed).

Core & Recomp Plan

Post-partum and asthma-safe. No equipment needed.

3 Days Active

Mandatory Warm-up (3 Mins)

Arm circles (10 forward/back), gentle torso twists, 10 slow deep breaths expanding the ribcage.

1

Full Body & Deep Core

  • Incline Push-ups (Hands on chair) 3 x 10 reps
  • Bodyweight Squats 3 x 15 reps
  • Stomach Vacuums (Belly to spine) 3 x 5 holds
  • Glute Bridges 3 x 15 reps
2/4

Active Recovery

Gentle 20-30 minute walk with the kids.

Keep a conversational pace to protect asthma and aid recovery.

3

Posture & Agility

  • Reverse Lunges 3 x 10 / leg
  • Bird-Dogs (On hands & knees) 3 x 10 / side
  • Wall Sits 3 x 30 sec
  • Dead Bugs (Protect lower back) 3 x 10 reps
5

Core & Leg Power

  • Step-Ups (On low chair/step) 3 x 12 / leg
  • Modified Side Planks (On knees) 3 x 20 sec / side
  • Heel Taps (Lying on back) 3 x 15 reps
  • Stomach Vacuums 3 x 5 holds

Weekends (Days 6 & 7) are full rest days. Prioritize sleep and hydration.
