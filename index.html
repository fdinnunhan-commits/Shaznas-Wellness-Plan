Why Sri Lankan Cooking Hides Calories

Sri Lankan food is naturally rich in vegetables and spices, but traditional prep methods hide hundreds of fat calories in oils, grated coconut, and thick coconut cream. Eliminating these 5 hidden traps cuts 400-500 calories daily without reducing food volume!

1. The "Thel Dala" (Tempering) Fix

-200 Cal

The Trap: Pouring oil straight from the bottle for curry bases adds 2-3 tbsp (250+ calories) per pot.
The Fix: Measure exactly 1 teaspoon of oil per pot. If onions stick, add a splash of water to steam-deglaze.

2. Zero Grated Coconut in Mallum

-180 Cal

The Trap: Mixing half a cup of grated coconut into Gotukola or Mukunuwenna adds high dense fats.
The Fix: Make mallum with red onions, diced tomatoes, green chillies, salt, and lime juice ONLY.

3. Thin Coconut Milk (Vathura Kiri)

-150 Cal

The Trap: Thick first-squeeze coconut cream (miti kiri) adds ~250 cal per half cup.
The Fix: Use only 2nd/3rd squeeze thin milk or water. Thicken curries by mashing a piece of potato or dhal.

4. No Pre-Frying Ingredients

-220 Cal

The Trap: Deep-frying eggplants, fish, or boiled eggs before currying turns them into oil sponges.
The Fix: Air-fry/bake eggplant, poach fish directly in Ambul Thiyal gravy, and soft-boil eggs.

Gastritis Prevention Protocol

  • Never Skip Breakfast: Eat within 45 minutes of waking to absorb overnight gastric acid build-up.
  • Warm Liquid Dinners: Soupy dhals, broths, and Kiri Hodi digest effortlessly, preventing nighttime reflux.
  • Natural Antacids: Use Goraka, Fenugreek seeds (uluhal), and papaya daily to soothe digestion.

Sunday 90-Minute Batch Prep Workflow

Step 1: The 50/50 Carb Base (15 Mins) Cook 4 cups red rice. Finely shred 1 large cabbage head and steam lightly for 6 mins. Store in separate containers in the fridge.
Step 2: Volume Protein Curries (40 Mins) Batch-cook Chicken/Beef curry with chopped Snake Gourd (Pathola) cooked directly into the meat gravy. Batch-cook Soya Meat in thin milk gravy.
Step 3: Soothing Dhal Base (20 Mins) Boil 2 cups red lentils with extra water, turmeric, ginger, and pandan leaves into a soupy broth. Store in a tub.
Step 4: Snacks & Aromatics (15 Mins) Boil 2 cups Green Gram (Mun Ata), cube 1 fresh Papaya, and pre-dice red onions and tomatoes for fast morning egg scrambles.

Workout Exercises & Video Form Guides

Incline Push-ups (Chest & Arms)

3 Sets x 8 - 12 Reps | Rest 60s

Upper Body

Hands on a sturdy chair, bench, or low wall. Keep body in a straight line, lower chest to edge, and push up. Builds posture to reduce double chin appearance.

Bodyweight Squats (Legs & Glutes)

3 Sets x 12 - 15 Reps | Rest 60s

Lower Body

Feet shoulder-width apart. Push hips back as if sitting in a chair. Keep chest up and exhale on the way up.

Stomach Vacuums (Deep Corset Core)

3 Sets x 5 Holds (10 sec each) | Rest 30s

Mummy Pooch Fix

Stand tall, exhale all air completely, and pull belly button back into the spine as hard as possible. Hold for 10 seconds while keeping lungs empty.

Glute Bridges (Hips & Lower Back)

3 Sets x 15 Reps | Rest 60s

Posture Rehab

Lie on back with knees bent. Drive through heels to lift hips toward ceiling. Squeeze glutes at top to fix post-partum pelvic tilt.

Bird-Dogs (Core & Balance)

3 Sets x 10 Reps per side | Rest 60s

Core Stability

On hands and knees with flat back. Extend right arm forward and left leg straight back simultaneously without arching lower back.

Dead Bugs (Lower Abs)

3 Sets x 10 Reps per side | Rest 60s

Safe Post-Partum Abs

Lie flat on back with knees at 90 degrees. Lower opposite arm and leg toward floor while pressing lower back firmly into the floor.

Reverse Lunges (Legs & Balance)

3 Sets x 10 Reps per leg | Rest 60s

Knee-Safe Legs

Step backward with one leg and lower hips until both knees bend to 90 degrees. Stepping back protects knees and asthma breathing.

Wall Sits (Leg Stamina)

3 Sets x 30 sec holds | Rest 60s

Stamina Build

Press back flat against a wall and slide down until knees are at 90 degrees. Hold while maintaining slow, deep breathing.

Step-Ups (Glutes & Quads)

3 Sets x 12 Reps per leg | Rest 60s

Functional Strength

Use a sturdy low chair, bench, or bottom step of stairs. Step up with right foot, bring left foot up, and step down softly.

Modified Side Planks (Obliques)

3 Sets x 20 sec per side | Rest 60s

Waist Tighter

Lie on side propped on forearm with knees bent. Lift hips so body forms a straight line from head to knees. Tightens waist sides.

Heel Taps (Lower Abdominal Control)

3 Sets x 15 Reps | Rest 45s

Core Rehab

Lie on back with legs raised at 90 degrees. Slowly lower one heel to tap the floor while keeping core braced and lower back flat.
